# Carolina BioOncology Institute

> Carolina BioOncology Institute (CBOI) is a physician-owned Phase I oncology clinical
> research clinic and translational laboratory in Huntersville, North Carolina, founded by
> Dr. John Powderly II. It runs first-in-human and early-phase immunotherapy trials for
> patients with advanced solid tumors, and operates the Human Applications Lab (HAL) — a
> cGMP-capable cell-processing and biorepository facility developing autologous cellular
> therapies — alongside RUO analytical and clinical lab analysis services. CBOI is the
> parent company of BioCytics.

## API surface

Carolina BioOncology Institute publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDK and
no machine-readable contract**. This was established by probe, not assumption:

- `https://carolinabiooncology.org/openapi.json` — 404
- `https://carolinabiooncology.org/swagger.json` — 404
- `https://carolinabiooncology.org/api-docs` — 404
- `https://carolinabiooncology.org/graphql` — 404
- `https://carolinabiooncology.org/llms.txt` — 404
- every named `/.well-known/` path (security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json, agent.json) — 404
- `api.carolinabiooncology.org` and `developer.carolinabiooncology.org` — do not resolve

The one machine-readable surface on the domain is the stock WordPress REST API discovery
document at `https://carolinabiooncology.org/wp-json/` (HTTP 200, 610 routes across
`wp/v2`, `oembed/1.0`, `contact-form-7/v1`, `yoast/v1`, `aioseo/v1`, `google-site-kit/v1`
and other plugin namespaces). That is content-management infrastructure emitted by the CMS
and its plugins, not a product API the company designed, documented or supports, so it is
deliberately NOT registered as an API of this provider.

CBOI is an openEHR **Industry Partner** — an adopter and coalition member of the openEHR
health-record specifications, not a publisher of them. That affiliation is why this company
entered the API Evangelist harvest backlog; it does not imply a CBOI-operated openEHR
endpoint, and no such endpoint was found.
